Surface-gradient shader and weathered-rock.js adapted from Tidewater
Grass coverage handover and cloud density/self-shadowing studied from
src/world/vegetation/GrassField.js and the current default src/sky/SkyProClouds.js.
The density construction in island-clouds.js is adapted from SkyProClouds.js.
tools/bake-cloud-weather.mjs ports its periodic Perlin weather FBM to JavaScript;
cloud-weather.bin is the locally generated linear density map. Solar disc
transmittance follows the same cloud-opacity threshold.
The prototype uses a bounded GLSL cloud renderer and a locally
generated periodic noise volume, rather than Tidewater's WebGPU temporal renderer.
shore.js and water.js: swash-front film thinning, horizontal foam bead/trail
widths and analytic front coverage adapted from src/ocean/ShoreWaves.js and
src/ocean/WaterMaterial.js at the fixed commit below. The local analytic cycle
uses its own derivative to classify uprush/backwash.
Dual-phase local flow advection follows src/ocean/SurfFoam.js (1.2-second
overlapping phases); water absorption uses the refracted-depth path from
src/ocean/WaterMaterial.js. The source creek and marsh confluence are locally authored.
Water extinction / in-scattering and unresolved-variance GGX equations in water.js are adapted from
src/ocean/WaterMaterial.js; this prototype uses analytic wave packets, not its FFT solver.
ocean-spectrum.js: dispersion and JONSWAP envelope adapted from src/ocean/OceanFFT.js;
64-mode quadrature into two WebGL2 slope maps, not the original FFT implementation.
Rock source: src/world/terrain/RockGeometry.js
Fixed commit: 4811ba48d795197de5621985f404e765c0b7c0ef
Surf lace: tools/bake-surf-lace.mjs is adapted from src/ocean/SurfFoam.js at
4811ba48d795197de5621985f404e765c0b7c0ef; runtime water.js samples its distance field.
