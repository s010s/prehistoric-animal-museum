# Static ecology assets

Original geometry: prehistoric-animal-museum, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.
Rock texture derivative: Poly Haven Rock 01, CC0; source URLs and hashes in
`../lookdev-materials/manifest.json`. No direct sunlight is baked into albedo.

See `../PROVENANCE.md` and the local manifest for the source recipe, metre-scale
bounds, LOD counts and texture budget. Trees at all distances share one source
identity. The four terrain material trials are not globally enabled.
